Problem & Data Contract

Forecast B concentration and reactor temperature at 1/5/15/30 min ahead using 60-min past + 30-min future control plan.

Input

Past (60,6)

frac_A, coolant_setpoint_C, concentration_A, concentration_B, reactor_temperature, jacket_temperature

Input

Future Controls (30,2)

frac_A (feed fraction) + coolant_setpoint_C — known in advance by the operator

Output

8-Dim Forecast

4 horizons × 2 targets = [B@1,T@1,B@5,T@5,B@15,T@15,B@30,T@30]

Constraint

No Leakage

Windows built per run_id. Standardizer trained on train only. No cross-experiment pooling.

Conclusions & Recommendations

Key Findings

  • LSTM_improved (BiLSTM-Attention) is the best model: NRMSE=0.0984, 82.7% skill over persistence, 97.2% high-temp warning recall at 15-min.
  • XGBoost_improved is a strong, fast alternative (NRMSE=0.1062, 98s training). Use for rapid retraining when process conditions change.
  • 30-min B concentration remains hardest — operators should apply wider tolerance at long horizons.
  • Off-spec low-B warnings remain challenging (~52% recall). Supplement with direct concentration monitoring.
Rec 1

Adopt LSTM_improved

Use BiLSTM-Attention for 15/30-min forecasts. Best accuracy during control-change events.

Rec 2

XGBoost for Quick Checks

Retrain XGBoost_improved first (~98s) for rapid quality assessment before full LSTM pipeline.

Limitation

Advisory Tool Only

Not a safety system. Trained on anonymised historical data. Cross-check with physical sensors.
